1. Ford City Mall redevelopment heads to Plan Commission vote today

Chicago's Plan Commission votes today (10 a.m., City Hall Room 1103) on Kurv Industrial's request to rezone the shuttered Ford City Mall into a four-building industrial campus. Kurv broadened the plan in the final week — adding a Ford City heritage plaza and a new CTA bus station — pushing the investment to roughly $200M. The official agenda calls for four buildings up to 45 ft totaling ~1,092,572 SF, with 904 parking spaces, 216 loading spaces and 160 truck stalls. The project is expected to create ~185 permanent jobs plus ~90 construction jobs. Even if approved today, it still requires the Zoning Committee and full City Council.

2. Moving Archer Forward — adoption resolution on today's agenda

The Chicago Plan Commission is also slated to adopt the Moving Archer Forward corridor plan at the same Aug 20 hearing. The DPD/RTA plan covers a 6.5-mile stretch of Archer Avenue (Halsted to Cicero) and projects ~800 housing units, 1,200 residents, 280,000 SF of retail and 835 jobs over 10–20 years, anchored by 31 Equitable Transit-Oriented Development sites near CTA Orange Line stations. Public comment closed Aug 6.

LINKS Chicago Construction News • Chronicle Media • City of Chicago
3. Protesters rally outside City Hall ahead of Ford City vote

A 9 a.m. rally was organized outside City Hall ahead of today's 10 a.m. vote, with Southwest Side residents voicing concerns about industrial truck traffic and noise if the former West Lawn shopping center is rezoned from B3-2 community shopping to M2-2 light industry. Ald. Derrick Curtis (18th) backs the industrial plan; opponents, including a ~1,200-signature petition, have pushed for a mixed-use or transit-hub alternative.

LINKS CBS Chicago — Protests • Block Club Chicago
4. Chicago industrial market tightens — vacancy falls to 4.5%

JLL's Q2 2026 Chicago industrial report shows the market tightened again: vacancy fell to 4.5% as 10.6M SF leased for a fourth straight quarter above 10M SF, with net absorption of 5.8M SF and average asking rent of $8.12/SF. The tight Southwest Side industrial corridor — where Ford City's redevelopment and Sterling Bay's logistics buildout sit — continues to draw manufacturing and distribution demand.
