Six Corners’ long-stalled People’s Gas overhaul to break ground this summer — $120M, 251-apartment project is the “final piece” of the corridor’s revival

The $120 million redevelopment of the former People’s Gas site at 3955 N. Kilpatrick Ave. — more than six years in the making — is finally scheduled to begin construction in late summer, developer Prairie Ridge Development told Block Club. The Elmhurst-based firm, which bought the project from GW Properties in December 2024, plans 251 apartments spread across two buildings (down from the original 346) plus four one-story retail buildings. The retail phase is slated for completion by summer 2027, with renters moving in the following fall. It is the last major piece of the Six Corners corridor’s transformation, which has already delivered a new Target, Aldi, the Clarendale senior-living tower and the 206-unit 6 Corners Lofts — while Chicago’s first Pita Inn, a Burlington and a Wells Fargo are rising at the former Sears Auto Center one block east.
