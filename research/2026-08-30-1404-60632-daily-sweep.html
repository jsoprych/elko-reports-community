Ford City Mall redevelopment advances to City Council Zoning Committee

Kurv Industrial’s $200M plan to demolish Ford City Mall and build a four-building industrial campus (~1.09M SF, 45-ft, 904 parking / 216 loading / 160 truck stalls) cleared the Chicago Plan Commission on Aug 20 by a 6-yes / 6-abstain / 1-recusal / 1-no vote. The project now heads to the City Council Zoning Committee — expected “no later than October” — with closing anticipated by end-2026 and a ~2-year construction timeline (near end-2028). It would create ~185 permanent and ~90 construction jobs, plus a public heritage plaza, new CTA bus station and a reconstructed 76th Street.

1. LeClaire Courts redevelopment — $127M first phase set to break ground this fall

Funding is secured for the $127M first phase of the LeClaire Courts redevelopment at 4809 W 44th St in Garfield Ridge (along Cicero Avenue, adjacent to Midway International Airport and Archer Heights). Led by Cabrera Capital Partners and The Habitat Company with SCB as architect, two connected six-story buildings will deliver 183 residential units (~90% affordable, including CHA vouchers), a 134-vehicle parking garage, a 10,000 SF childcare facility, 2,200 SF of commercial space and two playgrounds. The funding stack sources $33M in LIHTC, $27M in CHA loans, $9.5M in city funds and $5.5M in TIF; groundbreaking is approved for fall 2026.

2. Moving Archer Forward corridor plan up for formal adoption

The DPD + RTA-led plan for the 6.5-mile Archer Avenue corridor (Halsted to Cicero, spanning Archer Heights, Bridgeport, Brighton Park, Garfield Ridge and McKinley Park) went before the Chicago Plan Commission for formal adoption on Aug 20. The plan projects roughly 800 housing units, 1,200 new residents, 280,000 SF of retail and 835 jobs over 10-20 years, anchored by 31 Equitable Transit-Oriented Development sites (~13 acres) near CTA Orange Line stations. Public comment closed Aug 6; adoption clears the way for city departments to begin implementation.
