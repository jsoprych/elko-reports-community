Antonelli Wins Monza from 19th as F1 Heads to the All-New Madring

Kimi Antonelli charged from the back of the grid to win the Italian Grand Prix, passing team-mate George Russell with three laps to go to become the first Italian home winner in 60 years. With the championship leader now 66 points clear, F1 heads to the brand-new Madring in Madrid for the Spanish Grand Prix (Sep 11-13) — the first race in the Spanish capital since 1981.

Overview

Kimi Antonelli delivered one of the great Italian Grand Prix drives, charging from 19th on the grid to win at Monza and become the first Italian home winner in 60 years — his seventh victory of a dominant season that now sees him 66 points clear of Mercedes team-mate George Russell. Charles Leclerc's heavy lap-two crash and a mere sixth place for Lewis Hamilton made for a chastening home weekend for Ferrari, whose ADUO engine upgrade failed to deliver. This weekend the championship heads to the all-new Madring in Madrid, where F1 returns to the Spanish capital for the first time since 1981 — a 5.47km, 22-corner street-and-permanent hybrid featuring the banked 'La Monumental' corner, and a step into the unknown for every team.

This Week in F1 History

A look back at what happened around this time (early September) in Formula 1 history.
Sep 4, 1966
Scarfiotti's home win — Italy's last at Monza (until now)
Ludovico Scarfiotti won the 1966 Italian Grand Prix for Ferrari at Monza — the most recent time an Italian had won the race until Kimi Antonelli's victory from 19th on the grid this weekend, exactly 60 years on.
Sep 5, 1971
Gethin wins F1's closest-ever finish at Monza
Peter Gethin's BRM beat Ronnie Peterson's March by just 0.01 seconds at the 1971 Italian Grand Prix, with the top five covered by 0.61s — still the closest finish in Formula 1 history.
Jun 21, 1981
Villeneuve's Jarama masterclass — F1's last race in Madrid
Gilles Villeneuve won the 1981 Spanish Grand Prix at Jarama, his sixth and final victory, holding off a five-car train by 1.24s. It was the last time F1 raced in Madrid — until this weekend's Spanish GP at the new Madring, 45 years later.
