Leclerc Tops Practice as F1 Returns to Sepang for the Bahrain GP in Malaysia

The relocated Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia (Oct 2-4) marks F1's return to Sepang for the first time since 2017, with the race on Sunday. Charles Leclerc topped FP2 for Ferrari ahead of Isack Hadjar and Lando Norris, after Max Verstappen led FP1. It follows George Russell's lights-to-flag Azerbaijan GP win, which cut Kimi Antonelli's championship lead to 66 points. Qualifying is Saturday, the race Sunday — then F1 heads to Singapore (Oct 9-11).

Overview

F1 touches down at Sepang for the relocated Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia (Oct 2-4) — the sport's first visit to the Malaysian circuit since 2017. Charles Leclerc topped Friday's second practice for Ferrari ahead of Red Bull's Isack Hadjar and McLaren's Lando Norris, while Max Verstappen set the pace in FP1. It follows a dramatic Azerbaijan Grand Prix, where George Russell won lights-to-flag in Baku to cut Kimi Antonelli's championship lead from 81 to 66 points after the Italian crashed in qualifying and recovered to fifth. Lando Norris suffered a costly DNF, and Verstappen's back-to-back podiums have put Red Bull firmly back in the fight.
