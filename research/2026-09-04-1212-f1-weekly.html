This Week in F1 History

A look back at what happened around this time (early September) in Formula 1 history.
Sep 4, 1960
Ferrari 1-2-3 at Monza — a front-engined finale
At the 1960 Italian Grand Prix, Phil Hill led a Ferrari one-two-three ahead of Richie Ginther and Willy Mairesse on the combined Monza road course and banked oval. It proved the final World Championship win for a front-engined car.
Sep 4, 1966
Scarfiotti's home win — Italy's last at Monza
Ludovico Scarfiotti won the 1966 Italian Grand Prix for Ferrari, the most recent time an Italian has won at Monza. That same day Jack Brabham sealed his third title — the first driver to win the championship in a car bearing his own name.
Sep 4, 2009
'Crashgate' erupts
The FIA announced charges against Renault after Nelson Piquet Jr alleged he was told to crash deliberately at the 2008 Singapore Grand Prix. The scandal eventually led to the exits of Flavio Briatore and Pat Symonds.
