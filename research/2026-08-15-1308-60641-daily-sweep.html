🏗️ Six Corners Peoples Gas Overhaul Finally Set to Break Ground This Summer — $120M Prairie Ridge Plan

The long-stalled redevelopment of the former Peoples Gas site at Six Corners (3955 N. Kilpatrick Ave.) is finally cleared to start construction in late summer 2026. Elmhurst-based Prairie Ridge Development — which bought the entitled project from GW Properties in December 2024 — will build roughly 251 apartments across two mid-rise buildings plus four one-story retail buildings, an estimated $120 million investment. The redesigned plan trims density from the original 346-unit concept and will be built with union labor, with retail expected to deliver by summer 2027 and renters moving in the following fall. At least 20% of units (about 50) will be affordable under the city's ordinance. Ald. James Gardiner (45th) has called the project 'the final piece of the puzzle' for the Six Corners shopping district's revival.
