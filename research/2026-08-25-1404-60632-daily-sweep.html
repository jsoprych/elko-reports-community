🏗️ Ford City Mall Rezoning Advances to City Council — $200M Industrial Campus

The Chicago Plan Commission approved Kurv Industrial's plan to demolish the shuttered Ford City Mall and replace it with a four-building, ~1.09M SF industrial campus on Aug. 20 (6 yes, 6 abstain, 1 recusal, 1 no). The $200M project is now formally advancing to the City Council's Zoning Committee — expected no later than October — ahead of a full Council vote, despite continued opposition from some West Lawn neighbors.
