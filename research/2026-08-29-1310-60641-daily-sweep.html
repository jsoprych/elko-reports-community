🏗️ $120M Peoples Gas Site Overhaul at Six Corners Set to Break Ground This Summer

After more than six years and a dozen design iterations, the long-stalled redevelopment of the former Peoples Gas site at 3955 N. Kilpatrick Ave. is finally scheduled to begin construction in late summer. Elmhurst-based Prairie Ridge Development — which bought the project from GW Properties in December 2024 — is planning a roughly $120 million development with about 251 apartments and retail, with the first-phase retail targeted for summer 2027 and move-ins expected by fall 2028. It is the latest piece of a transformation that has already added more than 800 new residential units to the Six Corners district, alongside a Target-anchored apartment complex, a 258-unit senior living community, and a wave of new retail including Chicago’s first Pita Inn.
