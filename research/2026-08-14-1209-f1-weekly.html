This Week in F1 History

A look back at what happened this week (Aug 7-13) in Formula 1 history.
Aug 10, 1986
First Hungarian Grand Prix
The Hungaroring hosted the first-ever F1 race behind the Iron Curtain. Nelson Piquet's Williams-Honda beat Ayrton Senna's Lotus in a legendary duel, watched by 200,000 spectators.
Aug 14, 1994
Schumacher wins in Hungary
Michael Schumacher took victory at the Hungaroring for Benetton, tightening his grip on the 1994 title battle against Damon Hill.
Aug 15, 1982
De Angelis' first win — by 0.05s
Elio de Angelis claimed his maiden F1 victory at the Austrian GP (Österreichring), beating Keke Rosberg by just 0.050 seconds — one of the closest finishes of the era.
