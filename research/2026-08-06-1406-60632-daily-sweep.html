1: Moving Archer Forward Plan Heads to Commission for Final Approval

The Moving Archer Forward corridor plan advances to the Chicago Plan Commission for a decisive vote. The 5.5-mile complete streets vision covers Archer Avenue from 47th to Western, proposing protected bike lanes, wider sidewalks, improved crosswalks, and traffic-calming measures. The plan has drawn both passionate support from pedestrian safety advocates and organized opposition from the “Archer Avengers” coalition, who argue bike lane installation will worsen congestion for the corridor’s 28,000 daily drivers. If approved, implementation begins in phases starting 2027.

3: Amazon Building Southwest Side “Last Mile” Facility Amid Community Concerns

Amazon is constructing a new last-mile delivery facility on Chicago’s Southwest Side, drawing mixed reactions from residents concerned about truck traffic, diesel emissions, and displacement pressure on existing industrial tenants.
